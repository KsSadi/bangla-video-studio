# Style: Motion Graphics / Explainer (scene-plan.json)

- Label each cue group: hook, statement, steps, number, comparison, call to action. One per scene.
- Hook and call to action: big `title` (+ `sub`). Steps: `list` or `icons` with one item per spoken step, timed with
  `at`. Numbers: `chart` (`bar` for comparing, `line` for change over time, `progress` for percentages) with
  `highlight` on the value being discussed. Concepts: `icons` or an `illustration`.
- Cool, confident palettes; `grid` suits tech and data, `particles` suits ideas. Use `zoom`, `wipe` and
  `slide-up` for energy, `fade` only for calm moments.
