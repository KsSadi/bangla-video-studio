# Style: Cartoon Story (scene-plan.json)

- The character is the star: present in most scenes, `side` alternating, `center` for emotional beats.
  Emotion changes with the story (worry `thinking`, joy `happy`, surprise or victory `excited`, narration `explaining`).
- Scenes follow story beats: setting, problem, attempt, turn, resolution, moral. One beat per scene.
- Visuals: `illustration` and `icons` as story props (`Home`, `Trees`, `Sun`, `Moon`, `Bird`, `Fish`, `Crown`,
  `Coins`, `Heart`), a short `title` for the place or the key line, `list` only for the moral.
- Warm palettes (`particles` for outdoors or magic, `gradient` for calm), soft transitions (`fade`, `slide-left`
  in the direction the story travels), `zoom` on the turning point. Charts almost never fit.
